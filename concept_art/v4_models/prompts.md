# Model style exploration v4: towers and enemies

Three candidate styles for the Blender models. Each style gets two sheets: the three towers, and the four enemy types.
Generated with Codex CLI (gpt-6-astra, reasoning high, fast service tier).

## Shared direction (every image)

- Soft stylized realism, the mood of a calm miniature-world indie game. Warm daylight, soft long shadows, gentle ambient occlusion, matte believable materials, subtle tilt-shift feel.
- NOT cartoon, chibi, mobile-game, toy plastic, clay or glossy. No thick outlines, no oversaturated colours.
- Model-sheet layout: objects stand on a small patch of soft green grass on a plain warm light-grey studio backdrop, evenly spaced, three-quarter view from slightly above, so the shapes read clearly for 3D modelling.
- Simple, chunky, readable silhouettes that a 3D artist can model as low-poly game assets. Tree trunks and timber stay dark.
- No text, labels, logos or watermarks. 16:9 landscape.

## Towers sheet (same style family across the three)

1. Cannon tower: a sturdy round tower about 8 m tall with an open fighting deck on top and a swivelling gun on a turntable.

3. Sniper tower: tall, slim lookout about 11 m tall with a long-barrelled gun on a small turning stand at the top.

## Enemies sheet

Four small soldier types side by side, each about 1.8 m tall, each with its own team colour on the cloak or cloth only (body stays muted):
1. Infantry (faded crimson): shield and spear, plain.
2. Engineer (ochre): wooden plank or beam on the back, hammer in hand.
3. Climber (muted violet): lean, hunched, coil of rope and grappling hook on the back.
4. Boatman (teal blue): wide hat, carrying an oar; next to him a small rough timber rowing boat.

## Style A: Japanese-inspired (wa)

Towers on curved dry-stone ishigaki bases with dark timber watch-houses, small grey tiled roofs with gently upturned eaves, tall vertical nobori banners in faded indigo. The cannon is a dark bronze barrel on a timber carriage. Enemies read as ashigaru foot-soldiers and mountain bandits: jingasa hats, short lamellar armour, straw sandals.

## Style B: European pastoral

Round rubble fieldstone towers with timber decks and railings, weathered wooden shingle roofs, moss at the base, faded blue square cloth banners, warm lantern light. The cannon is a cast-iron barrel on a timber carriage. Enemies read as medieval raiders: hoods, padded jackets, cloaks.

## Style C: Blended fantasy

Fieldstone and timber construction like B, but with tiled roofs with slightly upturned eaves and long vertical cloth banners, cherry-blossom pink accents. The cannon is a bronze barrel with simple decorative bands. Enemies mix hoods and conical straw hats with cloaks, clearly a fictional faction.
