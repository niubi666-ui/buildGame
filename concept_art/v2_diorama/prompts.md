# God-Mode Tower Defense: concept art v2 (clean miniature diorama)

Mockups of in-game screenshots for a 3D tower defense game where the player is a god who sculpts terrain.
The attached reference screenshots show the desired RENDERING STYLE ONLY. Do not copy their sand, beach, sandcastles, UI icons, assets or layout.

## Shared art direction (apply to every image)

- Must look like a real-time capture from a small, polished indie 3D game. It must not look like a painting, illustration, poster or photo.
- The whole level is a miniature diorama: a rectangular slab of land seen from above, with the cut edge of the soil visible along the slab's borders and a plain soft warm-grey void outside it.
- Camera: high three-quarter view with a slightly tilted horizon and a toy-like miniature feel. The whole slab is mostly in view.
- Terrain is the star: smooth, soft, continuous sculpted surfaces with broad rounded forms, like modelling clay. Slopes shade smoothly. Use large clean color fields with very gentle color variation.
- Materials are matte and simple. No high-frequency texture, no photographic rock detail, no heavy outlines.
- Lighting: clean soft daylight, one sun, simple soft cast shadows, gentle ambient occlusion. No dramatic sunset, no lens flare, no bloom, no fantasy glow, no haze.
- Palette: fresh and bright but natural. Soft meadow greens, warm tan earth, muted grey-brown rock, dark green simple pines.
- Water is the most beautiful element: clear turquoise that deepens to rich blue with depth, a pale foam line at the shore, soft ripples, and the underwater terrain visible through it.
- Vegetation is simple and low-poly: clumps of small rounded bushes and simple cone pines, scattered in natural groups, with lots of open empty ground between them. Keep the image uncluttered.
- Dug ground shows two or three broad soft earth bands: dark brown, ochre, then grey. Keep the bands smooth and simple.
- Player towers: chunky, simple, rounded stone-and-wood miniatures with a small blue flag and few details. They must read clearly at small size.
- Enemies: very small, simple toy-like figures with solid red clothing, in small groups, with no fine detail. Bridges and boats are simple chunky wood.
- Home base: a small cluster of simple rounded toy-like houses with a low stone wall, at one end of the slab.
- UI is minimal: a row of simple cream rounded-square icon tiles at the bottom and a small pill-shaped counter at the top left. Avoid readable words, logos and watermarks.
- Aspect ratio 16:9 landscape.

## 01_build_phase.png: build phase, sculpting

No enemies. A green valley slab with two soft rounded mountain ridges on the long sides and a tan dirt path down the center toward the home base. A deep rounded pit is being dug into the path, showing soft earth bands. A thin, simple white circular brush ring rests around the pit. A smooth flat terrace has been shaped on one ridge's slope, with a faint pale grid marking it as buildable. A thin dashed red line shows the predicted enemy route, which leaves the path and curves around the pit.

## 02_battle_phase.png: battle phase

The same slab during battle. Four chunky towers stand on smooth terraces on the ridge slopes, firing simple small projectiles at the valley. Pale translucent patches on the ground show each tower's reachable area, clearly cut off where a ridge blocks its line of sight. Small groups of red figures walk along the path. Some are building a simple wooden bridge across the deep pit. There is no brush ring, because terrain editing is locked during battle.

## 03_enemy_decisions.png: enemy route decisions

A closer view of part of the slab, showing four route choices at once. Red figures walk down through a shallow soft-edged dip and up the other side. Others build a wooden bridge over a deep steep pit. Others row small boats across a turquoise lake with clear depth gradients. A thin line of others walks around the base of a tall smooth newly raised hill. Each group has one small simple floating icon above it (footprints, hammer, boat, curved arrow), with no text.

## 04_late_game.png: late game, reshaped valley

The whole slab after several rounds, in the same clean daylight. The land is obviously sculpted by the player: smooth terraces with towers, a long winding trench, a tall new hill, and a dug basin flooded into a beautiful turquoise lake connected by a channel. Two simple wooden bridges remain from earlier rounds. Small red groups approach from the far end while the home base stands at the near end. It remains calm, clean and easy to read, with plenty of open ground.
