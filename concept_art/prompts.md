# God-Mode Tower Defense: concept art prompts

These are concept mockups of in-game screenshots for a 3D tower defense game. The player is a god who sculpts terrain.

## Shared art direction (apply to every image)

- Must look like a real-time 3D PC game screenshot, not a painting poster and not a photo.
- Stylized realism: physically based materials with slightly simplified, hand-crafted forms, clean large shapes, rich but controlled color, soft global illumination, atmospheric depth. Comparable in spirit to high-end stylized strategy games.
- Setting: a temperate alpine valley with grey-brown granite, green meadows, dark pine forests, a clear turquoise-blue lake, and a small walled medieval village at the far end of the valley (the player's homeland).
- Camera: elevated strategy camera, about 45 degrees down, long lens feel, whole battlefield readable. Units are small compared with the terrain.
- Lighting: low warm sun from the left, long soft shadows, cool blue shade, light haze in the distance.
- Sculpted terrain is the star. Dug ground reveals layered strata: dark topsoil, ochre clay, then grey rock. Raised ground has fresh rock faces with soil and grass at the edges.
- Player defenses: stone and timber towers with blue banners and a subtle cyan glow.
- Enemies: human-scale raiders in dark leather with crimson banners and capes, clearly readable against the terrain.
- UI is minimal, clean and semi-transparent, with thin gold lines. Use icons and numbers rather than words. Avoid readable words, logos and watermarks.
- Aspect ratio 16:9 landscape.

## 01_build_phase.png: build phase, sculpting

Calm before the battle, no enemies present. A wide valley between two tall mountain ranges, with a dirt road running down the center toward the walled village. A glowing thin golden circular brush ring with soft falloff rests on the valley floor, where a large deep pit is being dug into the road, showing layered strata. On a mountainside, a freshly carved flat terrace is ready for a tower, with its flat area outlined by a faint green grid. A dashed glowing crimson line predicts the enemy route: it runs along the road, then diverts around the pit. A small faded bridge icon hovers at the edge of the pit. Small HUD: a gold coin counter at top center, and a row of terrain tool icons (raise, lower, smooth, flatten, pinch) at the bottom.

## 02_battle_phase.png: battle phase, towers firing

The same valley during battle, in the same style. Several stone and timber towers stand on carved terraces on the mountainside, firing glowing bolts down at the valley. Translucent cyan range domes show range cut off by terrain, leaving clear blind spots behind a ridge. Columns of crimson raiders march through the valley. Sparks, dust and small fires are visible. The large pit from the build phase remains in the road, and raider engineers are building a rough timber bridge across it. The golden terrain brush is absent, because terrain editing is locked during battle. HUD: wave counter pips, gold coin counter and tower icons.

## 03_enemy_decisions.png: enemy route decisions

A closer strategy view, showing three route choices at once in one continuous landscape. On the left, raiders walk down into a shallow pit and climb up the other side. In the middle, raider engineers build a timber bridge across a deep steep-sided chasm with exposed strata, and others wait in a queue. On the right, raiders paddle rough wooden boats across a turquoise lake. In the background, a tall newly raised mountain has a thin line of raiders walking around its base. Each group has a small floating icon above it (footprints, hammer, boat), with no text.

## 04_late_game_sunset.png: late-game valley

A dramatic wide view of the same valley after several waves, at golden hour. The land is now clearly reshaped by the player: terraces cut into mountains, a deep trench, a new mountain, an artificial lake flooding a dug basin, and enemy-built bridges left standing from earlier rounds. Many towers defend the walled village in the foreground. A large crimson raider army approaches in the distance. Epic scale, but still readable as a strategy game camera view. Minimal HUD.
