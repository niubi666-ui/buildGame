# God-Mode Tower Defense: concept art v3 (Tiny Glade-inspired soft realism)

Mockups of in-game screenshots for a 3D tower defense game where the player is a god who sculpts terrain.
The attached video frames show the desired RENDERING STYLE ONLY: lighting, materials, softness and miniature depth of field. Do not copy their buildings, composition or watermarks.

## Shared art direction (apply to every image)

- Must look like a real-time capture from a beautiful, grounded indie 3D game with a soft, tactile, miniature-world realism, similar in mood to Tiny Glade.
- NOT a mobile game. Not cartoon, chibi, toy plastic, clay, fuzzy felt, flat color or glossy. No oversaturated green. No thick outlines. Not a detailed fantasy painting either.
- Lighting is the main feature: warm, low, directional sun; long, soft, natural shadows; rich bounced light and soft global illumination; deep but gentle ambient occlusion; warm highlights and slightly cool shade.
- Miniature photography feel: subtle tilt-shift depth of field, with the nearest and farthest areas softly blurred and the battlefield in focus.
- Terrain is the star, with large naturally sculpted slopes. Ground is covered in dense, fine, combed grass that follows the terrain and flows softly with visible strands up close. Palette: muted olive, straw gold, dry amber and soft autumn tones.
- Trees: rounded, soft canopies made of many small leaf clusters, in muted russet, ochre and olive, with soft self-shadowing, gathered in natural groves.
- Rock and soil: believable matte materials with restrained detail. Dug ground reveals natural soft layers of dark topsoil, ochre clay, then grey stone, with crumbling irregular edges and small stones. No clean painted stripes.
- Water: calm, believable and softly reflective, with deep muted teal-blue, a transparent shallow edge, and subtle shoreline ripples. Not neon turquoise.
- Player towers: handcrafted, small, believable fieldstone and timber watchtowers with weathered shingled roofs, slightly irregular stonework, moss at the base, warm lantern light and a small faded blue cloth banner.
- Enemies: tiny, believable human figures in muted dark clothing with faded crimson cloaks and banners, in proportion to the buildings. Not chibi and not bright red.
- Wooden bridges and boats: rough, believable, weathered timber, structurally connected.
- Home base: a small, cozy village of a few stone-and-timber houses with shingled roofs, chimney smoke, warm windows and a low irregular fieldstone wall.
- The world fades softly into warm mist at its edges. No hard slab edge.
- UI is minimal and quiet: thin, semi-transparent warm-grey elements with few small monochrome icons. Avoid readable words, logos and watermarks.
- Aspect ratio 16:9 landscape.

## 01_build_phase.png: build phase, sculpting

Calm, no enemies, golden morning light. A wide valley between two large sculpted ridges, with a pale dirt path through the grass toward the village. A deep natural pit is being dug into the path, with crumbling edges and exposed earth layers. A thin soft-white circular brush ring lies on the ground around it. On a ridge slope, a smooth flat terrace has been shaped, with a faint thin white grid. A subtle dashed faded-crimson line predicts the enemy route around the pit. The UI shows only a small row of terrain tool icons and a small gold count.

## 02_battle_phase.png: battle phase

The same valley during battle, in late-afternoon light. Stone watchtowers on carved terraces fire small arrows and stones with no glowing magic. Faint pale tinted light on the terrain shows each tower's reachable area, with obvious gaps where a ridge blocks its line of sight. Columns of tiny raiders walk the path, while others build a rough timber bridge across the deep pit, with dust and a thin plume of smoke. There is no brush ring. The UI shows only tower icons, with no terrain tools.

## 03_enemy_decisions.png: enemy route decisions

A closer, lower miniature-photography angle with stronger depth of field and rich grass detail. In one continuous landscape, tiny raiders use four routes: walking down through a shallow grassy dip, building a timber bridge over a deep rocky pit, rowing rough wooden boats across a calm lake, and filing around the base of a tall freshly raised hill. Small subtle monochrome icons (footprints, hammer, boat, curved arrow) float above the groups.

## 04_late_game.png: late game, reshaped valley

A wide view of the valley after several rounds, with warm sunset side light and long tree shadows. The land has clearly been reshaped: stepped terraces with watchtowers, a long winding trench, a tall new hill, and a basin flooded into a calm lake. Two weathered timber bridges remain from earlier rounds. Tiny raiders approach from far away, while the cozy village with warm windows sits in the foreground. Keep plenty of open grassland so it stays easy to read.
