# Model style exploration v5: strongly different styles, monster enemies

Four styles that differ in material, shape language and palette, not just roof shape. Every style has its own monster faction; no human enemies.
Generated with Codex CLI (gpt-6-astra, reasoning high, fast service tier).

## Shared direction (every image)

- Soft stylized realism in calm daylight: soft long shadows, gentle ambient occlusion, matte believable materials, miniature-world feel. Daytime only, no glowing night lanterns.
- NOT cartoon, chibi, mobile-game, toy plastic, clay or glossy. No thick outlines, no oversaturated colours. Monsters are creatures of a believable little ecosystem, not cute mascots and not gory.
- Model-sheet layout: objects stand on a small patch of grass on a plain warm light-grey studio backdrop, evenly spaced, three-quarter view from slightly above, so shapes read clearly for 3D modelling.
- Simple, chunky, readable silhouettes that can be modelled as low-poly game assets. No people anywhere in the image.
- No text, labels, logos or watermarks. 16:9 landscape.

## Enemies sheet

Four monster types side by side, all from the same faction, each roughly 1.5 to 2 m tall. Each type carries its team colour as a clear patch (markings, shell, crest or cloth) on an otherwise muted body:
1. Basic walker (faded crimson): sturdy, plain, the common foot soldier of the faction.
2. Bridge builder (ochre): carries or produces material to span gaps.
3. Climber (muted violet): lean, many-limbed or clawed, built to scale cliffs.
4. Boat rider (teal blue): shown next to or riding the faction's small boat or raft.

## Style A: Overgrown ruin

Towers are ancient grey standing stones and carved monoliths wrapped in living tree roots, thick moss and pale lichen; the weapons are dark timber bound with vines and rope. Organic, leaning, asymmetric shapes. Palette: moss green, lichen grey, dark bark.
Monster faction: bramble and bark creatures. Walker: a stumpy bark-skinned creature with thorn spikes. Bridge builder: a broad beaver-like creature hauling logs. Climber: a long thin stick-insect creature with hooked limbs. Boat rider: a frog-like creature on a curled leaf raft.

## Style B: Porcelain and brass

Towers are smooth white glazed ceramic forms with cobalt-blue painted patterns, brass fittings, gears and green-patina copper caps. Clean, elegant, rounded, symmetric shapes. Palette: white, cobalt blue, warm brass.
Monster faction: armoured insects. Walker: a heavy rounded beetle. Bridge builder: a large ant carrying twigs and silk. Climber: a slender long-legged gecko-like creature. Boat rider: a water-strider insect skating beside a hollow seed-pod boat.

## Style C: Basalt and forged iron

Towers are stacked dark hexagonal basalt columns with heavy forged black iron, rivets and chains, with faint warm forge glow in seams. Angular, heavy, faceted shapes. Palette: charcoal, rust, ember orange.
Monster faction: moss and mud golems. Walker: a squat lumpy mud-and-stone golem with moss on its back. Bridge builder: a wide golem with flat slab arms that lays stone planks. Climber: a slim crystal-clawed rock lizard. Boat rider: a round mossy golem in a hollowed log canoe.

## Style D: Windmill canvas and pale wood

Towers are light pale timber frames with cream sailcloth, windmill-like vanes, kites, pulleys and ropes, on low whitewashed stone footings. Airy, tall, open, lightweight shapes. Palette: cream, pale ash wood, soft sky blue.
Monster faction: burrowing beasts. Walker: a stout armoured mole-like creature. Bridge builder: a badger-like digger dragging planks. Climber: a long-clawed pangolin-like creature. Boat rider: an otter-like creature in a little bark coracle.
